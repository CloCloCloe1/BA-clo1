# BA User SOP

## Purpose

This system is used to record store activity for consignment products, including tester requests, damages, theft, and restock requests. Product information is pulled from the product master list after the BA enters or scans a barcode.

[APP LINK]

## Account Access

BA users can either use an assigned account or register with the company registration code.

To register:

1. Open the app link.
2. Click `Register BA`.
3. Enter the registration code provided by the company.
4. Create your username and password.
5. Enter your BA name.
6. Enter the store, such as `Miniso` or `TNT`.
7. Enter the location, such as `BRO` or `STL`.
8. Click `Create BA account`.
9. Go back to `Sign in` and log in with your new account.

## Navigation

After login, BA users will see:

- `Record`: submit tester, damage, theft, or restock records.
- `Search`: search the product master list.

BA users do not have access to the Admin page.

## Submit A Record

1. Go to `Record`.
2. Select the record type:
   - `Tester`
   - `Damage`
   - `Theft`
   - `Restock`
3. Confirm the date.
4. Enter quantity.
5. Confirm BA name.
6. Confirm store, for example `Miniso` or `TNT`.
7. Confirm location, for example `BRO` or `STL`.
8. Scan the full barcode or enter the last 6 digits.
9. Select the correct product if multiple products appear.
10. Add notes if needed.
11. Click `Save record`.

After saving, the button will briefly show a saved state to prevent duplicate submissions.

## Edit Today's Entries

At the bottom of the `Record` page, BA users can see `Today's entries`.

You can edit records submitted by your own account on the current day.

Editable fields:

- Date
- Type
- BA Name
- Qty
- Store
- Location
- Notes

Locked fields:

- Last6
- Full Barcode
- Product Name
- Brand
- Category

Product fields are locked to protect the product master data. If the wrong product was selected, submit a corrected record and notify Admin.

## Product Search

1. Go to `Search`.
2. Search by:
   - Product keyword, such as `serum` or `mask`
   - Full barcode
   - Last 6 digits
   - Brand
   - Category
3. Use the category filter if needed.

## Important Notes

- Always check the product match before saving.
- Do not submit the same item multiple times unless it is truly a separate record.
- Use notes for anything Admin should know.
- If you made a mistake today, edit it in `Today's entries`.
- If you made a mistake from a previous day, contact Admin.
